\section{News layer, regime ablations, and the fitted-constant registry}
\label{app:news}

\paragraph{News design rules.}
On our own event days, news publication and market reaction are
simultaneous within one 15-minute GDELT indexing cycle: the March 23 Iran
story was first indexed at 12:00 UTC, the same hour ES swung $+2\%$. Two
rules follow. The LLM can never front-run headlines, and the LLM never
sees market data: market moves only trigger runs.

\paragraph{Schedule.}
Checkpoints run at 02:30, 04:30, 07:00, 08:00, 08:35 (mandatory,
post-release) and 09:15, plus an event trigger on any $0.15\%$ gap move
within 15 minutes; LLM runs are capped at $6+6$ per night. The calendar
itself is thin: BLS blocks scripted schedule fetches, so without a FRED
key only the NFP first-Friday rule applies, flagging 20 of 465 days.

\paragraph{Group A: midnight briefing.}
At 00:05 an LLM briefing reads the calendar, earnings, nowcast-versus-
consensus and prediction-market structural odds; measured over 30 days,
structural odds move only about 0.2 points overnight, so one snapshot
stays fresh. Output is a variance multiplier $m_A \in [1, 2.5]$ anchored
at the fitted 1.29, and a directional tilt $\lvert d_A \rvert \le 0.3$
allowed only on nowcast--consensus disagreement. The primary model is
GitHub Models gpt-4.1, about 12 calls per night with 1--2K-token payloads.

\paragraph{Group B: fitted effects.}
Two deterministic effects are fitted from data. After a gap move of at
least $0.35\%$ in one hour within the last two hours, the official-open
residual is wider: pooled ratio 1.90 over 76 shock days, with unstable
halves. On conflicted days, where a shock happened but the gap sits under
$0.15\%$, the shock's sign predicts the official open 85.4\% of the
time ($n = 41$), encoded as $\mu_B = 0.9\,d_B\,c\,\sigma_m$ with the 0.9
chosen conservatively; the term is zero by construction on already-repriced
days. For the strategy backtest these constants were refit train-only
(shock 1.32, conflict 0.85) after the audit described in
Appendix~\ref{app:execution} caught pooled-fit look-ahead.

\paragraph{Regime ablations.}
Tercile-$k$ without the widened regime sigma is worse than baseline (7
overconfident misses), and the fitted release-morning multiplier of 1.29 is
small but right-signed (NFP-subset Brier 0.1993 to 0.1971). v1.3 sharpens the
early hours but is slightly worse late, so production runs v1.3 before 5:00
and v1.2 after. Quirk days stay beyond futures data: v1.2 keeps 4 of 12
safe, right or no-bet at 9:00 and the same as baseline, but softens wrong-side
confidence (Mar 10 from 84\% to 66\%, Apr 9 from 84\% to 70\%), and at 9:00
no wrong call at 80\% confidence or above remains. Extending the fit window
to 465 days degrades late-hour calibration because the train half then
swallows the tariff-chaos regime; the 200-day numbers remain the validated
reference.
