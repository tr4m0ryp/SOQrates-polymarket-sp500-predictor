\label{sec:conclusion}

We measured one assumption the Polymarket crowd makes every night: that the
official S\&P~500 open realizes the full overnight futures gap ---
$k = 1$, $\sigma = 0$ \parencite{polymarket2026spxmarket}. It does not, and
it keeps not doing so. Only about 80\% of the gap survives into the first
tick, fitted $k \approx 0.77$--$0.80$ with $a_0 = 0.0092\%$, and a frozen
linear model built on nothing but that mismeasurement climbs from 72.5\%
direction accuracy at midnight to roughly 94\% at 9:29 over 200
out-of-sample days.

The edge is real, and it is small. After fees, spread, and impact, the
hour-4 hold turned \$100 into \$135.92 on the test half at a tenth of the
bankroll per trade, and the first-signal rule reached \$850.69 at 26W--2L
under the compounding stake $\min(\tfrac{1}{2}\,\text{balance},\ \$400,\
1\%\ \text{of day volume})$. That last term is the ceiling. The
1\%-of-volume clamp holds effective stakes near \$1{,}000 per day, so the
strategy cannot outgrow the market it trades, and capital beyond that point
buys almost nothing.

Nothing here is unconditional. The evidence spans one regime and 58 test
days, taker fees arrived mid-sample, volume is falling, and the crowd can
learn. Therefore monthly re-validation is part of the method, not
maintenance around it. What runs in production is one rule: brief at 00:05,
enter as taker at the first hour in 4--9 where the edge reaches 5 cents at
probability 0.70 or higher and price at most 95 cents, stake by the
min-rule and skip thin days, monitor at 08:35 and 9:00, re-check live
futures at 9:28, refit monthly.

The next step is not more accuracy from the same inputs. Futures data is at
its floor. The identified step is the auction replica on real NOII and
imbalance data --- metered Nasdaq NOII history plus the free academic NYSE
TAQ feed, validation at zero cost and roughly \$17--40 per month live ---
which, at the measured sample-day sharpness, would move quirk days from 2
of 12 strictly called on futures alone to a majority called. The machinery
is built and validated on synthetic days. Feeding it real ones is the work
that remains.
