## 3. Core market model (frozen, validated)

19. Core formula: predicted official gap = a0 + k * ES_futures_gap; P(up) = Phi((a0 + k*gap)/sigma(tau)). Fitted a0 = 0.0092 (%), pooled k ≈ 0.767–0.797 (config K_DEFAULT = 0.767); the official open realizes only ~80% of the futures gap. [memory:spx-open-predictor-roadmap.md] [repo:config.py] [repo:docs/approach.tex]
20. Width law: sigma(tau)^2 ≈ 0.0066 + 0.0002*tau (% units, tau = minutes before 9:30, fitted intraday). Empirical hourly sigma: 0.344% at 00:00 → 0.30% at 04:00 → 0.15% at 09:00 → 0.083% at 9:29. [memory:spx-open-predictor-roadmap.md]
21. Out-of-sample direction accuracy (200 trading days, Sep 2025–Jul 2026): midnight 72.5%, 04:00 77.0% (154/200), 06:00 82%, 07:00 85%, 09:00 90%, 9:29 ~94% (n=18 minute-level). [memory:spx-open-predictor-roadmap.md] [transcript:618d0dce]
22. Ceiling with perfect 9:30 market data: 96.3% — the remainder is auction noise (approach.tex appendix: fitted from 81 unseen days). [memory:spx-open-predictor-roadmap.md] [repo:docs/approach.tex]
23. Coin-toss filter: dropping days with midnight confidence <65% (44% of days; 88 of 200 excluded) lifts midnight accuracy to 82.1% (92/112). The filter IS the trading rule (CONF_COMMIT = 0.65). [memory:spx-open-predictor-roadmap.md] [repo:config.py] [transcript:618d0dce]
24. Sigma decomposition: pure mapping/auction noise measured at ±0.08–0.09% (the 9:29 floor); the rest of the midnight ±0.34% is future-news variance sqrt(0.34^2 − 0.09^2) ≈ ±0.33% — ~94% of midnight uncertainty is events that have not yet occurred. A perfect per-stock model at midnight would add only ~1–2 accuracy points. [transcript:618d0dce]
25. Even at 9:29 the futures error floor is ±6 index points — the auction mechanism itself. On Jun 22 (official −0.14 pts) every model version said +1.7 to +5.7: inside the floor, unpredictable from any 9:29 price data. [transcript:618d0dce] [repo:docs/approach.tex]
26. Directional flip risk formula (drift of the effective gap): center drifts ~0.014% per sqrt(minute); P(flip) = Phi(−|0.83*g| / (0.014%*sqrt(tau))). Example: +0.40% gap at 7:00 has ~1% flip risk; +0.10% gap at 7:00 has ~30% flip risk. [transcript:618d0dce]

## 6. News layer

Design rules: the LLM can never front-run headlines, and the LLM never sees market data (market moves only trigger runs). [memory:spx-open-predictor-roadmap.md] [repo:docs/approach.tex]
